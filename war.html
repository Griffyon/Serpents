Torn Targets – War

Log in with your Torn API key and your FFScouter key to receive war targets without Discord.
Get Torn key
Torn → Settings → API Keys. A public-access key is enough.
Get FFScouter key
FFScouter must be activated for your account first, following the FFScouter instructions.
Your keys are sent over HTTPS to the Torn Targets server and stored encrypted. They are never saved in this browser.
